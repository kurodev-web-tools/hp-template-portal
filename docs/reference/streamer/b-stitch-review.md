## Verdict
- This Stitch output is usable as a `visual base` for template B
- It is materially stronger than the first Stitch attempt
- It should not be implemented as-is
- The correct use is:
  - adopt the atmosphere and composition cues
  - revise the wording, streamer specificity, and practical content structure before implementation

## Why It Improved
- The hero now communicates `confrontation` instead of a luxury dossier UI
- The distant ruler silhouette makes the room feel occupied by a dominant presence
- The metrics section is no longer a flat dashboard grid and better supports the `dominance` concept
- The system vocabulary is closer to `Boss Room` than the previous output

## What To Keep

### 1. Hero Composition
- Keep the distant ruler / throne silhouette concept
- Keep the centered lock-on framing
- Keep the large negative space and visual pressure
- Keep the top-level `INITIATE ACCESS` style CTA direction

### 2. Atmosphere
- Keep the black / violet / cold-metal palette
- Keep the restrained glow approach
- Keep the sharp edges and thin reticle accents
- Keep the cinematic, premium, non-generic tone

### 3. Metrics Structure
- Keep the asymmetrical hierarchy
- Keep one dominant primary metric with smaller supporting metrics
- Keep the oversized typography for the lead figure

### 4. Schedule Framing
- Keep the `access window` framing instead of a plain calendar
- Keep the sense of restricted access / opening slot

## What Must Change Before Implementation

### 1. Make It More Clearly A Streamer Site
- The current output still leans too far toward an abstract fashion-brand or authority-brand page
- Add more concrete streamer-facing information:
  - stream genres
  - signature content formats
  - audience participation style
  - realistic schedule entries
  - platform-specific access paths

### 2. Replace Over-Abstract Copy
- Current labels such as:
  - `THE SOVEREIGN`
  - `THE PRESENCE`
  - `Strategic Conquest`
  - `Void Whispers`
  - `Executive Strike`
  are visually effective but too abstract for a reusable streamer template
- Replace them with language that remains world-flavored but maps to real streamer content

### 3. Reduce Residual Brand-Site Feeling
- The current composition feels premium, but at times more like a luxury campaign page than a streamer homepage
- Add stronger signals of ongoing activity:
  - stream cadence
  - content categories
  - viewer entry points
  - archive / channel context if needed

### 4. Rework The Desktop Side Navigation
- The side navigation is acceptable as a mockup device
- It should not be carried directly into implementation without simplification
- For real implementation:
  - reduce structural weight
  - ensure mobile-first fallback
  - avoid depending on a desktop-only fixed rail for meaning
